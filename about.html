About Agualine Laundry

Agualine Laundry was conceptualized to address the heavy reliance on manual, paper-based record-keeping in local laundry and dry-cleaning small-and-medium enterprises (SMEs).

By shifting to our digital web application, businesses eliminate garment mix-ups, missing paper slips, and delayed tracking—delivering total peace of mind to our customers.
