How Our Service Works

1. Book or Drop Off

Contact our team to request a pickup or bring your garments directly to our laundry counter.

2. Garment Registration

Our staff record your customer details, garment information and order number to help prevent mix-ups.

3. Washing and Processing

Your garments are sorted, washed, dried and finishedaccording to their care requirements.

4. Order Tracking

Our team updates your order as it moves through the processing stages: Received, In Progress and Ready.

5. Payment and Delivery

Confirm your bill, pay usingLipa na M-Pesa,Paybill No 166116 and collect your laundry or arrange delivery.

Our Service Options

Drop-Off Service

Bring your laundry to our premises at a convenient time.Our staff will register your order and explain the expected completion time.

Pickup Service

Request collection of your laundry from your home or workplace. Pickup availability depends on your location and our schedule.

Delivery Service

Once your garments are ready, arrange delivery to your preferred address within our confirmed service area.

Service Coverage and Delivery Times

We serve customers within our designated local service area.Contact us to confirm whether your location is covered, available pickup times, delivery charges and expected turnaround.

Delivery schedules may vary depending on order size, garment type, workload and distance.

Payments and Order Information

  1. Receive an order reference for easier order identification.
  2. Confirm the laundry charges before making payment.
  3. Use M-Pesa when the payment service is available.
  4. Keep your payment confirmation for reference.
  5. Contact our staff if you have questions about your bill or order status.

M-Pesa STK Push payments can be supported through ourintegrated payment system when configured. Do not share your M-Pesa PIN with anyone.
